ABOUT SWAM YEE

Professional engineering experience since 2009.

SWAM YEE began operating as an Engineering Services Group in 2009, providing Electrical Systems, Plumbing & Sanitary Systems, ELV Systems, Fire Fighting Systems and M&E installation services.

In 2014–2015, SWAM YEE Efficient Engineering Group Co., Ltd. was officially established with the main objective of providing professional and reliable Mechanical & Electrical engineering services.

Our engineering works are supported by experienced engineers, technicians and specialists with practical experience across different M&E systems.
