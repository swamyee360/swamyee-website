About SWAM YEE

WHO WE ARE

Engineering experience built since 2009.

SWAM YEE began operating as an Engineering Services Group in 2009, providing professional engineering services in Electrical Systems, Plumbing & Sanitary Systems, ELV Systems, Fire Fighting Systems and other related Mechanical & Electrical works.

During our early years, we also carried out M&E installation works as a subcontractor. Our team has worked on projects under established main contractor companies including Hexagon, Wonder World and FEVS.

In 2014–2015, SWAM YEE EFFICIENT ENGINEERING GROUP CO., LTD. was officially established under Company Registration No. 4893. The company's main objective is to provide reliable and professional Mechanical & Electrical engineering services.
